[One Star, Written in Sap](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-one-star-written-in-sap-2ea1f4f4):
one line, under 280 characters, in the voice of a houseplant leaving a
1-star review of its human owner.

> One star: my owner Googles 'why is my plant sad' while holding the
> watering can he hasn't used since March.

The grievance is the plant's own — unwatered since March — and the joke
is the owner searching for the cause while holding the cure he never uses.
